## 4. Methodological Safeguards

1. **Strict Boundary Defense**: Prompt injection strings in customer inquiries cannot override evaluation rubrics.
2. **Deterministic Caching**: All LLM judge outputs are cached in `evaluation/judge_outputs.json` to guarantee reproducibility without credit waste.
3. **Segregated Offline Rubric**: The local rule-based heuristic is strictly designated as an `Offline Rubric Sanity Check` and is barred from headline LLM metrics.
